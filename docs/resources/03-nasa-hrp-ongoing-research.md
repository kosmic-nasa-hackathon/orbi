## Summary

Catalog of HRP research with humans, in three blocks: **Artemis II** (deep space), **low Earth orbit (ISS)** and **past research**. It serves as evidence of what is actually measured in astronauts today and how.

## Artemis II (deep space)

| Study | What it does | Fact sheet |
|---|---|---|
| **ARCHeR** | Monitored astronauts' **well-being, activity and sleep patterns** to study health and performance in deep space. | [link](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/archer/) |
| **Immune Biomarkers** | Analyzed **blood and saliva** samples to see how the immune system changes in deep space. | [link](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/immune-biomarkers/) |
| **Artemis II Standard Measures** | Crews contributed a **consistent** set of health information to a data bank for future researchers. | [link](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/artemis-ii-standard-measures/) |

## How we use it in ORBI

- **ARCHeR validates our capture model:** NASA already monitors **well-being, activity and sleep** on Artemis II. The watch that records sleep and the mood check-in are not invented. Cite it in "How it is used on board" and in the pitch.
- **Standard Measures validates the cadence:** NASA collects a *consistent* set of physiological **and psychological** measures. It is the idea behind our fixed daily check-in. *"Like NASA's Standard Measures, ORBI asks for the same core set every day, so drift becomes visible."*
- **Tempus Pro is the closest precedent:** a device that integrates health data for remote diagnosis. ORBI goes one step further: it **decides on board** when the remote response takes 14 minutes.
- **Immune Biomarkers / Altered Immunity** support the Immune system and the early-infection correlation.
- **Zero T2** (exercise without a treadmill) supports exercise adherence in Musculoskeletal: in deep space the exercise equipment will be limited.
- **B-Complex, Brain Fluid Pressure, Thigh Cuff and Spinal Fluid Biomarkers** confirm that SANS is an active area: a good argument for the **Amsler test** at Level 2.
- **Space Gardening** serves as a behavioral countermeasure idea in an action card ("time in the plant habitat").
