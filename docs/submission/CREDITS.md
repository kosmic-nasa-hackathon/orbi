ORBI is a NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 project by team Kosmic. The source code is MIT-licensed;
the material below keeps its original terms.

## NASA and CSA

The product uses summaries and evidence from official NASA and Canadian Space Agency material:

- NASA Human Research Program: the five hazards of human spaceflight, risks, ongoing research and
  bone-fracture evidence.
- NASA JSC: space-radiation monitoring and the Space Radiation Analysis Group.
- Canadian Space Agency: radiation monitoring, the CANS neutron detector and the rationale for
  science in space.
- NASA/NSBRI and the scientific literature on women's health in spaceflight and ground training.

The source list and links are in [`../resources/`](../resources/README.md). NASA and CSA material
is attributed to those agencies; ORBI does not claim ownership of it.
